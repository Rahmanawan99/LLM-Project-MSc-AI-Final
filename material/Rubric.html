CSIP5501 MSc Project Assessment Marking Grid 

 

A distinction level overall means that the candidate must: 

  • demonstrate an authoritative grasp of the conceptual context within which the work was undertaken; 

  • display originality, insight and powers of in-depth critical analysis in the solution offered and/or is able to sustain an argument displaying originality, insight into current debates and conceptual position, in-depth critical analysis, and expresses this argument clearly, concisely and accurately; 

  • demonstrate a high degree of relevant technical competence.

Problem Framing 

Understanding of Problem & Requirements 

 

Little understanding of the problem domain, resulting in a substandard product in most respects. 

Some understanding of the problem domain has been demonstrated, gained through limited research, analysis and design, but insufficient to meet the standard expected for a pass. 

An acceptable understanding of the problem domain, gained through research, analysis and design but lacking insight or flair and discrimination.  

A good understanding of the problem domain, gained through well referenced and evaluated research of current literature, sound analysis and design.  

An excellent critical understanding of the problem domain, gained through comprehensive, fully referenced and evaluated research of current literature, thorough analysis and design. 

Outstandingly excellent critical understanding of the problem domain, with original insights, gained through comprehensive, fully referenced and insightfully evaluated research of current literature. 

Research questions 

No consideration of how the work can be used to find anything out or make a scholarly contribution. 

Some consideration of how the work might answer questions, but research questions are vague or unclear or infeasible to answer. 

Some thought given to how to use the work to answer questions. Questions are limited and basic or lack sophistication. 

Some sensibly chosen research questions, showing some intelligent thought about how the work can make a scholarly contribution. 

Good set of well-formulated research questions that are addressed by the programme of work, showing knowledge and a clear path for the work to make a scholarly contribution. 

Broad range of clearly formulated research questions that are coherent, imaginative, insightful and instructive, showing sophisticated thought about the domain. The programme of work effectively addresses the questions. 

Facts & Lit Rev Review 

Literature review & fact-finding  

Little effort to investigate topics beyond the curriculum; or some investigation that is inadequately referenced. 

Some investigation of topics beyond the curriculum, but poorly understood and described, or with sloppy referencing. 

Limited investigation of topics beyond the curriculum, adequately understood and explained, with adequate referencing making consistent use of a standard reference format, possibly with some gaps or misunderstandings.  

Good systematic investigation of selected topics beyond the curriculum, with good survey of appropriate sources, clearly understood and well described, and with good referencing making consistent use of a standard reference format.  

Very good systematic investigation and critical analysis of selected topics beyond the curriculum, with thorough survey of appropriate sources, fully understood and well described, with immaculate referencing.  

Outstandingly good background research into a wisely selected range of topics beyond the curriculum, showing good awareness of the state of the art, critical analysis and with immaculate referencing. 

Research analysis 

Little attempt to organize or interpret research materials, or with major and disastrous misunderstandings. 

Limited or seriously flawed attempt to organize and synthesize background research material, with some misunderstandings or not connected to the work of the project. 

Sensible attempt to synthesise background research material into a structured story, maybe with minor misunderstandings. Limited use to support development or research. 

Good understanding of material organized into a structured story, with application of background research to support development or research. 

Coherent analysis of background research material, with insightful application to support development or research. 

Background research material is fully understood, insightfully described, and synthesized into a coherent view, with clear implications for the project. 

Research and evaluation 

Primary Research and Development 

Very little or no evidence of critical analysis or systems design skills resulting in a sub-standard report or product. 

Poor critical analysis and/or little evidence of planning or sensible system design resulting in a substandard report or product. 

Work shows some evidence of critical analysis and evaluation and/or a reasonable attempt at system design resulting in a product/report meeting some of the requirements in the terms of reference.  

Very good work which demonstrates the student’s ability to synthesise material and construct responses, and which reveals good critical analysis and evaluation and/or a good system design using appropriate methodologies.  

Excellent work which demonstrates the student’s insight and in-depth critical analysis and/or a highly effective thorough systems design skills employing all appropriate methodologies. The project fully met or exceeded the requirements as recorded on the terms of reference. 

Outstanding work which demonstrates thorough and sophisticated understanding and effective use of research methods and/or system development techniques employing all appropriate methodologies. Very high-quality results. 

Critical Evaluation 

Critical review 

Very little or no attempt to assess either the project as a whole or what the student has found or produced. 

Some attempt to evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of the project and what it has produced, but inadequate or with some foolish views. 

Limited attempt to evaluate the deliverables and the project as a whole. Major gaps or insufficiently critical views. 

Good attempt to evaluate the project and the deliverables produced, but with some gaps or somewhat superficial in places. 

Insightful analysis of the strengths, weaknesses and failings of both the project as a whole and the deliverables produced. 

Excellent sophisticated analysis of the strengths, weaknesses and failings of both the project as a whole and the deliverables produced. 

Answers to research questions 

No attempt to consider what might be learned from the work. 

Some attempt to consider what could be learned from the work, but very limited or with serious misconceptions. 

Fair effort to draw more general conclusions from the project. May have some misconceptions or not be tightly linked to explicit research questions, or with inadequate consideration of the strength of evidence. 

Good effort to consider and answer research questions from the outcomes of the project, but with some limitations, e.g. lacking consideration of strength of evidence. 

Good thoughtful effort to answer research questions from the outcome of the project, considering the strength of the conclusions and the strength of the evidence. 

Excellent analysis, taking the outcomes of the work beyond the originally defined research questions, with insightful consideration of the nature of the conclusions and the strength of the evidence.

Dissertation & Doc 

Dissertation 

Very poor or seriously incomplete dissertation. 

Dissertation covers most required information but is poorly written, has major omissions, or is seriously unsatisfactory in other ways. 

Adequate dissertation covering all essential topics and presenting most of the required information in line with guidelines; reasonably readable and with a sensible structure. 

Good dissertation covering all essential topics, with no major omissions, and with clear explanations of most of the information required. Readable, well structured, and well presented. 

Very good, focused dissertation, with clear and thorough explanations of all the required information. Well written with no major deficiencies of English, and well presented. 

Outstandingly concise, focused dissertation, with clear and thorough explanations of all the required information. Well written with no major deficiencies of English, and well presented. 

Documentation 

None of the required documentation, or the documentation is seriously incomplete, or poor quality or incorrect. 

Documentation supplied but has major omissions or contains errors or major deficiencies or is very poorly presented. 

Adequate documentation, but with some omissions or significant limitations, reasonably clearly presented. 

Good, appropriate, reasonably complete documentation, clearly presented. 

All the required documentation, clearly and effectively presented. 

All the required documentation, clearly and effectively presented; (useful and informative) information beyond what we expect. 

Viva 

Demonstration / viva 

Very poor demonstration demonstrating little or no functionality, and unable to provide any defence of approach taken, or failing to demonstrate understanding of the system 

Content acceptable, but poorly planned demonstration. Able, with prompting, to answer a minority of the questions posed satisfactorily. 

Demonstration shows some insight into the student’s work OR shows limited functionality of the system. Reasonably well planned. Able to answer majority of questions posed. 

Competent performance with an acceptable guide. Demonstrated sound knowledge of the majority of the material relevant to the project. Satisfactory responses to majority of questions in the viva. 

Highly professional demonstration, with well-planned guide providing an excellent view of the project’s functionality and technical aspects. Logically presented with a thorough grasp of the relevant material. Good analysis of what project says about research questions.  

Exceptionally impressive and convincing demonstration, giving a well-planned presentation of the functionality and technical aspects. Giving clear, concise, focused, convincing analysis of what the project says about the research questions. 

Presentation / viva 

Very poor presentation demonstrating little or no knowledge of material relevant to the project and unable to provide any defence of approach taken. 

Content just acceptable but poor presentation skills OR Satisfactory presentation skills but knowledge of relevant material very patchy. 

Presentation provided some insight into the student's work but performance at the oral rather pedestrian. Able, with prompting, to answer several of the questions posed. 

Competent presentation using acceptable visual aids. Demonstrated sound knowledge of majority of material relevant to the project. Some consideration of what general lessons can be drawn. Satisfactory responses to majority of questions in the viva. 

Highly professional presentation with clear informative visual aids providing an excellent overview of the project. Clearly spoken with a thorough grasp of relevant material. Good analysis of what project says about research questions.  

Exceptionally impressive and convincing presentation with clear informative visual aids providing an excellent overview of the project. Clearly spoken with a thorough grasp of relevant material. Very good analysis of what project says about research questions. 

 

Project Management 

Poor project management: Inadequate time management, little or no contact with supervisor, late or missing documents. 

Inadequate project management. Serious deficiencies in time management, maintaining contact with supervisor, or getting documents produced. 

Mostly adequate project management, but with some problems with time management, proactively recognising and carrying out activities, maintaining contact with supervisor, or producing documents on time. 

Competent project management, Well organized and proactive with largely successful time management, sought contact with supervisor, produced required documents on time. 

Exemplary project management: Good time management; development well-organized, proactive and self-directed, while actively seeking and acting on advice from the supervisor. Timely production of required documents. 

Exceptionally good project management: Good time management; development well-organized, proactive and self-directed, while actively seeking and acting on advice from the supervisor. Timely production of required documents.
